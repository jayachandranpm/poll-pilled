# Central icon subset

These 11 SVGs are exact copies of the exported paths in `/Users/jaya-20646/Projects/Ant Trial/pollpool/central-exports.json`: home, chart, plus, wallet, user, search, image, check, down, close and audio. They use the source’s 24px coordinate system, line style and 2px stroke. Directional aliases rotate the source chevron.

The reference app obtained this subset from the official [Central Icon System](https://centralicons.com/) trial export UI by [Iconists](https://iconists.co/central). This is an application subset, not an icon-library distribution or a representation of a paid Pro license.
