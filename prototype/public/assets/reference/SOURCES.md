# Reference theme assets

- Outfit variable font: Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit. Font and license are hosted locally in `../fonts/`.
- Tabler outline SVGs: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons, MIT. Downloaded official 24px / 2px-stroke SVGs, mapped by `src/reference-icons.js`; license in `../tabler/LICENSE`.
- Empty-state illustrations are local, recolored derivatives of the prototype’s existing customized unDraw illustrations. Original shapes and artwork retained; palette adjusted to warm neutrals and green.
- Poll photographs and anonymous avatar assets are reused from the existing prototype.
- Wrenly screenshot supplied by the user is the visual reference; Poll Pilled retains its own product content and identity.
